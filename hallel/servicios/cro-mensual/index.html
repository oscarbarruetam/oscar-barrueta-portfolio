Continuous optimization areas

  • Funnel, Shopify Analytics, GA4 and customer behavior in Clarity (when available).
  • Home, collections, PDP, cart and mobile experience.
  • Merchandising, bundles, AOV, cross-sell and upsell.
  • Offers, promotions, shipping communication and trust.
  • Theme performance, Liquid/CSS/JS adjustments and QA.

What happens each month

  • Review business metrics, the purchase funnel and behavioral signals.
  • Select priorities through evidence, potential impact, effort and dependencies.
  • Design and implement the agreed Shopify improvements.
  • Validate the changes, measure their performance and record learning.
  • Use insights to choose the next priority; review progress and roadmap with the client.

When A/B testing belongs

Experimentation is optional and conditional, not the center of the service.

  • Enough traffic to make the experiment informative.
  • Reliable measurement and a valid hypothesis.
  • A suitable, compatible tool and technically sound implementation.
  • If those conditions are not met, prioritize other measurable improvements.

Service boundaries

  • Progressive redesign or reconstruction of store areas may be prioritized across months.
  • It does not equal completing a full CRO Redesign in one month.
  • Month-to-month collaboration; we recommend assessing the partnership across at least three months.
  • Full store builds, large migrations, paid media, full Klaviyo/email operations, paid app licenses and third-party costs are outside this service.

Important scope note: No guaranteed conversion lift, a fixed number of A/B tests or a full structural rebuild within one monthly cycle.
