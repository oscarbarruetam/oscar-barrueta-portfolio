01 / SETUP

Klaviyo Lifecycle Setup

For brands with no Klaviyo setup, incorrect configuration or underused lifecycle capabilities.

US$1,500
Build or repair your lifecycle foundation in a defined one-time project.
Discuss this scope ↗

Diagnosis and configuration

  • Initial Klaviyo audit.
  • Review of Shopify ↔ Klaviyo integration and available tracking.
  • Sender and domain authentication configuration or review, when applicable.
  • Basic deliverability assessment.
  • Initial list and segment cleanup or configuration.

Subscriber capture system

  • Review or create the primary sign-up form/pop-up.
  • Basic capture strategy and initial segmentation.

Up to six essential flows

Choose the relevant flows for the business model; the six below are examples, not an obligatory identical set.

  • Welcome Series
  • Abandoned Cart / Checkout
  • Browse Abandonment
  • Post Purchase
  • Winback
  • Back in Stock, Replenishment or a business-specific customer flow

Reusable email design system

  • Visual email structure aligned with client-provided branding.
  • One reusable email template and reusable blocks.
  • Responsive email design.

Base segmentation

  • Engaged and inactive audiences.
  • New and returning customers.
  • High-value / VIP customers.
  • Purchase behavior, when data allows.

QA and handoff

  • Check links, triggers, delays, conditions, responsive behavior and available tracking.
  • Documentation or summary of the configured system, with walkthrough.
  • Two adjustment rounds within the agreed scope.

Not included

  • Ongoing monthly campaign management or SMS.
  • Brand identity design, photography or continuous asset production.
  • Complex migrations from another ESP or highly customized automations.
  • Custom integrations or ongoing deliverability management.
  • Discounts or promotions funded by HALLEL.

Important scope note: Up to six flows are selected by business needs, available customer data and technical feasibility. Setup is a one-time project, not an ongoing campaign retainer.

02 / MONTHLY EMAIL

Klaviyo + Email monthly

For brands with a working Klaviyo foundation that need campaigns, flows, audience strategy and monthly analysis.

US$1,200 / month≈ $22,900 MXN / mes
Operate and improve lifecycle as a retention channel, with a defined monthly campaign capacity.
Discuss this scope ↗

Monthly strategy

  • Review key metrics and define a monthly calendar.
  • Prioritize campaigns, opportunities in existing flows and relevant segments.

Up to four campaigns per month

  • Campaign strategy and message structure.
  • Copywriting and assembly inside Klaviyo.
  • Design using the agreed visual email system.
  • Audience segmentation and scheduling.
  • Pre-send QA.

Flow optimization

  • Review flow performance, triggers, delays and timing.
  • Adjust segments, subject lines, content and offers when appropriate.
  • Update existing flows; additional creation or improvements depend on monthly capacity.

Audience segmentation

  • Engaged audiences and customers versus prospects.
  • Purchase behavior, high-value and inactive customers.
  • Category or product behavior when data supports it.

Testing when the volume supports learning

  • Test subject line, sender, send time, CTA, message or offer as appropriate.
  • No guaranteed number of A/B tests when the audience size is insufficient.

Monthly reporting

  • Klaviyo-attributed revenue, clearly identified as attribution rather than absolute causation.
  • Campaign and flow performance.
  • Open rate where informative, click rate, conversion and unsubscribe.
  • Deliverability indicators, findings and next priorities.

Not included

  • Full CRO/Shopify service, paid media or community management.
  • Customer support or SMS unless separately scoped.
  • Photography, continuous complex creative production or major custom integrations.
  • HALLEL-funded discounts, promotions or a guarantee of revenue.

Important scope note: Four campaigns per month is a maximum, not an unlimited production commitment. Monthly flow work depends on capacity and available data.

SERVICE CLARITY

Different scopes. Complementary value.
