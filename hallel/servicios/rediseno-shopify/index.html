01 / CRO SPRINT

Shopify CRO Sprint

The store's overall structure works, but one or two prioritized areas create a clear purchase obstacle.

US$1,200≈ $22,900 MXN
For one clear friction that does not require rebuilding the entire store.
Discuss this scope ↗

When a Sprint makes sense

  • Home and navigation
  • Collection pages and merchandising
  • Product detail page (PDP)
  • PDP and cart
  • Mobile UX
  • Bundles, AOV and cross-sell opportunities
  • Offer, promotion and its communication
  • One other high-priority CRO opportunity

How the intervention works

  • Diagnose the selected friction using available analytics and experience review.
  • Define one clear hypothesis or prioritized improvement brief.
  • Design and implement the agreed intervention in the relevant Shopify area(s).
  • Validate responsive behavior, navigation and core interactions before delivery.
  • Document what changed and what should be measured next.

Important scope note: This is not a cheap full redesign. It is a focused intervention, with the selected problem and deliverables defined before starting.

02 / CRO REDESIGN

Shopify CRO Redesign

US$2,500≈ $47,000 MXN

Discuss this scope ↗

Potential redesign areas

  • Commerce information architecture and navigation
  • Home and storefront hierarchy
  • Collection pages (PLP) and merchandising
  • Product detail pages and product education
  • Cart and pre-checkout journey
  • Mobile shopping experience
  • eCommerce components and commercial design system
  • Trust, reassurance and decision-making content
  • Offer and product presentation

Scope and delivery logic

  • Diagnose structural problems and determine which parts must change together.
  • Define the affected templates, components and commercial journeys in the project scope.
  • Create the experience system and implement the agreed storefront changes in Shopify.
  • Carry out responsive QA and verify key buying interactions.
  • Deliver a clear record of what was implemented and future measurement priorities.

Important scope note: The list describes possible components, not an automatic promise to redesign every part of the store. The affected areas are defined in the agreed project scope.
